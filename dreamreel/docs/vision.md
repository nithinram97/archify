# Vision: Hollywood quality, for anyone with a story

dreamreel is made in India for dreamers everywhere who want to make animated films that look and
sound like the best in the world, with little or no budget: one person, an ordinary laptop, free
software, free assets, their own language.

The budget is small. The standard is not. Every feature is judged against the frames of the best
animated films, and dreamreel does the expert work (modelling, rigging, acting, camera, lighting,
editing) so the film maker can spend their time on the story.

## Who it is for

- Story tellers without a studio: students, writers, YouTubers, teachers, kids, film makers.
- A laptop without a graphics card, sometimes patchy internet, often no money for software or AI.
- Stories in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, English,
  and in their own world: homes, streets, festivals, clothes, music.

## The quality bar

A film made with dreamreel should be able to sit next to a studio animated film without the viewer
noticing the difference in craft. Concretely, department by department:

| Department | The bar | Where dreamreel is |
|---|---|---|
| Story and editing | Clear staging, cutting on action, rhythm, J/L cuts | Strong: automatic director, edit list |
| Camera | Real lenses, framing, moves, the 180° rule, depth of field | Good; lens/sensor presets to come |
| Characters | Appealing designs, clean silhouettes, film-quality topology | Quaternius models (game quality); AI modeller started |
| Faces | Expressive brows, eyes and mouths; lip sync to the language spoken | Weak: the current gap |
| Body acting | Weight, timing, anticipation, contact with the world | Clips, procedural acting and posed gestures; needs AI motion |
| Sets and props | Rich, lived-in, culturally right places | Quaternius furniture, nature, city; Indian world kit to come |
| Lighting | Motivated light, mood per beat, colour script | Basic: time of day and weather |
| Look and render | Physically based shading, grading, motion blur, depth of field | EEVEE looks; a film-quality render path to come |
| Effects | Cloth, hair, water, fire, smoke | Rigid-body physics only |
| Sound | Voices in every Indian language, music, effects, mix | Piper voices (few Indian languages yet) |

The benchmark: re-making short films shot for shot and scoring each department against the
original (see the benchmark notes). A department is done when it scores as well as the original.

## Principles

1. **Quality first.** A feature isn't finished until it looks right in a render, not when its tests
   pass.
2. **Free at the core.** Open source (MIT), CC0 assets, free local AI. Paid services are optional,
   never required.
3. **Runs on what people have.** A CPU laptop renders a good film overnight; quality comes from smart
   craft (looks, lighting, timing), not brute force.
4. **The expert is built in.** The film maker says what happens; dreamreel knows how a professional
   would stage it, light it, cut it. They can always take control of any shot.
5. **Every language, every culture.** Voices, subtitles, interface and the world itself reflect the
   people telling the stories.
6. **Fits the professional world.** Films and previz export to studio formats (USD, FBX, EDL), so a
   dreamreel film maker can work with any studio.

## The road there

1. **Faces and lip sync**: expressive faces on every character, lip sync from the sounds of the line,
   in any language.
2. **Indian voices**: free, offline voices in Indian languages, with lip sync.
3. **The Indian world kit**: people, clothes, homes, streets, vehicles and things from India (CC0).
4. **Film-quality look**: a physically based render path with motion blur, depth of field, render
   layers and colour management; lighting that follows the mood of each beat.
5. **Performance**: AI motion from text and from a phone video of the film maker acting it out.
6. **Effects**: cloth, hair, water, smoke and fire.
7. **Studio exchange**: USD/FBX/EDL export, hand edits that dreamreel keeps.
8. **Made for where films are seen**: vertical video, bilingual subtitles, presets for YouTube,
   Instagram and OTT.
9. **A flagship film**: an Indian story, made entirely with dreamreel on a laptop, for nothing.
